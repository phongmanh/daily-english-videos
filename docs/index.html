DAILY ENGLISH

Daily English uploader

A small private tool that publishes the Daily English vocabulary videos (English words with pronunciation, meaning and examples for Vietnamese learners) to the Daily English YouTube channel and Facebook Page.

What it does

  • Creates short English-vocabulary videos.
  • Uploads them to the owner's own YouTube channel using the YouTube Data API.
  • Posts them to the owner's own Facebook Page.

It is used only by the channel owner. It does not offer sign-in to other people and does not collect data from viewers.
