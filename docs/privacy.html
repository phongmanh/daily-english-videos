Privacy policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

"Daily English uploader" (the "app") is a private tool used only by the owner of the Daily English YouTube channel and Facebook Page to publish their own vocabulary videos.

What the app accesses

  • YouTube: permission to upload videos to the owner's own channel (scope youtube.upload). The app uploads videos and sets their title, description, tags and publish time. It does not read viewers' data, comments, subscriptions or analytics.
  • Facebook: permission to publish posts and videos to the owner's own Page. It does not read data about people who view or interact with the Page.
  • TikTok: the owner's basic profile (user.info.basic) and permission to send videos as drafts to the owner's own TikTok inbox (video.upload). The owner reviews and posts each draft in the TikTok app. The app does not read other users' data, comments or analytics.

What the app collects

The app does not collect, store, sell or share personal data about anyone. There is no sign-in for other users and no tracking or cookies.

Access tokens

The access tokens that allow uploading are stored only as encrypted secrets in the owner's GitHub repository settings (the TikTok refresh token is also kept encrypted in the repository) and are used only to publish the owner's videos. They are never shared with third parties.

Google and YouTube

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Access can be removed at any time at Google Account → Third-party connections.

Removing access

To stop the app, remove it from your Google Account permissions (link above) and from Facebook Settings → Business integrations, and delete the secrets from the GitHub repository.

Contact

Questions: contact the Daily English channel through its YouTube page.
